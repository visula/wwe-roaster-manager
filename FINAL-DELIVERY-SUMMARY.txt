🎓 USAGE EXAMPLES
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Adding Your First Wrestler
──────────────────────────
  1. Click "Roster" tab
  2. Click "+ Add Wrestler"
  3. Enter: Name, Show (select from dropdown)
  4. Select: Division, Status
  5. Click "Save"

Scheduling Your First Match
───────────────────────────
  1. Click "Matches" tab
  2. Click "+ Schedule Match"
  3. Select: Show, Type, Date
  4. Enter: Participants (minimum 1)
  5. Click "Save"

Creating a Championship
──────────────────────
  1. Click "Championships" tab
  2. Click "+ Add Championship"
  3. Enter: Title name, Show, Current holder
  4. Set: Debut date
  5. Click "Save"

Recording a Transfer
───────────────────
  1. Click "Transfers" tab
  2. Click "+ New Transfer"
  3. Enter: Wrestler name, From Show, To Show
  4. Set: Transfer date
  5. Click "Save"
